How electrolysis works

  1. Pass a direct current through a molten or dissolved electrolyte to break it down.
  2. Positive ions move towards the negative cathode. Negative ions move towards the positive anode.
  3. At the cathode, reacting particles gain electrons (reduction). At the anode, they lose electrons (oxidation). These reactions form the products.
